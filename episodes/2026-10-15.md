# AI round-up, 15 October 2026

## 1. Harvard study: coding agents add code, not shipped output (today / research)
- Fiona Chen and James Stratton, Harvard working paper (not peer reviewed); Jellyfish telemetry, ~300M work events, 700+ firms.
- ~30% more lines of code, ~20% more commits, ~23% more PRs; Jira issue/epic resolution not significantly changed. PR time-to-merge +49%, revision requests nearly doubled, comments per PR +35%. ~80% of firms used AI code review but agents wrote ~23.3% of review comments.
- Sources (secondary): [Slashdot summarising Ars Technica](https://tech.slashdot.org/story/26/10/12/0234217/), [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/harvard-study-ai-coding-agents-lift-code-30-shipped-output-flat), [Four Week MBA](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-coding-agents-add-30-more-code-not-more-finished-work/), [Netzwoche](https://www.netzwoche.ch/news/2026-10-12/harvard-studie-stellt-produktivitaetsversprechen-von-ki-infrage), [Borncity](https://borncity.com/news/harvard-studie-zu-ki-agenten-pull-requests-brauchen-49-prozent-laenger/)
