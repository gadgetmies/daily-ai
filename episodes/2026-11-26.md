# AI round-up, 26 November 2026

Agents get official front doors to Workspace, Canva and Copilot connectors; Baidu opens ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview; OpenAI healthcare configurations; GDPx shows classifiers skewing scores; coding agents keep working after they've solved the task.

## 1. Google Workspace MCP servers and CLI go GA; agents must leave the REST APIs by 1 February 2027 — *actionable today*

Hosted MCP servers for Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Calendar, Chat and Tasks, plus an official Workspace CLI. A new Workspace AI Tools Addendum requires agents to use these instead of REST APIs, with enforcement from 1 February 2027 (REST-calling agents held to Standard quotas). Admin controls for agent access, confirmation rules and audit logs under Security > API Controls. Free allowance reported as 5,000 requests/day per product; scaled-tier billing reportedly from 1 April 2027. Confirmation covers sending, external sharing, trashing and deleting, not every write.

## 5. SemiAnalysis GDPx: safety classifiers are skewing benchmark scores — *research / further out*

GPT-6.2 Astra leads GDPx; Fable 5.2 second at 16.4%. With partial grading and classifiers removed, the gap narrows to 29.9% vs 32.2%; excluding cyber, biology and alignment, Fable 5.2, Opus 5.5 and GPT-6.2 Astra are ~tied around 54%. Gemini 4 Neon leads biology, likely due to more lenient classifiers. SemiAnalysis-reported, not independently replicated.

## 6. Coding agents solve early but stop late — *research / further out*

"Termination Lag" (arXiv 2611.14960): across five models, two frameworks and three SWE-bench sets, 38–82% of successful runs had a correct first edit; a median ~35–60% of steps came after the first correct patch; 1–8% of runs undid the correct fix. "Done, But Still Working" (arXiv 2611.22901): stopping at first passing self-tests saves 24–31% compute at up to 3.9 points accuracy cost; a learned stop rule saved ~30% with quality preservation uncertain. Mostly open models; abstracts and summaries read, not full papers.

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14960
- https://github.com/musacim/termination-lag
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22901
