# AI round-up, 14 October 2026

Figures below are as reported by the linked sources; most are secondary coverage or vendor claims.

## 1. Anthropic dynamic workflows in Managed Agents (actionable today)
Public beta from 9 October: an agent writes a program that runs up to 1,000 agents in phases, executed server-side as a workflow run. Reported limits: 64 concurrent, 24-hour default lifetime, 10 open runs per session. Vendor test: 66 of 70 hidden bugs vs 14-27 for a single agent.
- [Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/anthropics-claude-can-now-orchestrate-up-to-1000-ai-agents-in-parallel-through-dynamic-workflows/)
- [DEV Community](https://dev.to/albertomontagnese/anthropics-new-dynamic-workflows-let-agents-write-and-run-their-own-programs-6ig)
- [Kingy AI](https://kingy.ai/blog/claude-managed-agents-dynamic-workflows-beta/)
- [Latent Space](https://latent.space/p/claude-dynamic-workflows)

## 2. Sonnet 5.5 cache read price halved (actionable today)

## 3. Xiaomi MiMo-V2.6 and reward hacking in RL (research)
